Our Story

Hand-Crafted in Paradise SC

We bring you customised, unique crafting gifts hand-made with ❤️ in Seychelles. Founded by Christy Esparon, Pretty Dazzle is a celebration of local artistry and the pure joy of giving. What began as a personal passion for detailed crafting has blossomed into a dedicated gifting service.

Quality and presentation are at the core of what we do. We source beautiful materials, assemble each piece with precision, and package everything with the signature elegance that defines the Pretty Dazzle experience. When you choose us, you are not just purchasing a gift; you are investing in a memory.
