Our Story

Hand-Crafted in Paradise SC

We bring you customised, unique crafting gifts hand-made with ❤️ in Seychelles. Founded by Christy Esparon, Pretty Dazzle is a celebration of local artistry and the pure joy of giving. What began as a personal passion for detailed crafting has blossomed into a dedicated gifting service, designed to bring dazzling smiles to faces across the islands.

We believe that every gift should tell a unique, personal story. Whether you are celebrating a milestone birthday, a romantic anniversary, or simply surprising a loved one "just because," our creations are made to elevate those ordinary days into extraordinary moments. From our carefully curated luxury hampers bursting with treats to our vibrant, eternal artificial blooms, we pour our hearts into every single detail.

Quality and presentation are at the core of what we do. We source beautiful materials, assemble each piece with precision, and package everything with the signature elegance that defines the Pretty Dazzle experience. When you choose us, you are not just purchasing a gift; you are investing in a beautifully hand-crafted memory designed to last a lifetime.
